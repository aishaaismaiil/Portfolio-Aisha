The problem comes before the device

Someone can attend an activity without having access to the conversation, the group's pace or its decisions. SENSORA Social asks which barriers remain for people with deafblindness in situations they consider important.

Deafblindness does not always mean a complete absence of sight and hearing. Preferences, skills and communication methods vary. We do not assume that everyone reads braille or uses the same equipment.

What we know and what we don't

The 2022 review found few evaluated studies and no studies specifically focused on interventions for social life and support within its included sample. This does not prove that no solutions exist worldwide.

Related interventions exist, including the Get Together programme on multiparty communication (2026), Deafblind Hub and braille communication technologies. SENSORA's originality has not yet been established.

The project does not present a wristband, glove or platform as an already validated solution. Choosing an intervention depends on research and the participation of its intended users.

How to put the idea into practice

  1. Listen: identify specific social situations people want to change, using communication support they choose.
  2. Map: understand what people already use, what exists and where barriers remain.
  3. Co-design: choose a priority situation and develop an initial accessible trial in that context.
  4. Test: assess understanding, control over the pace, participation and perceived effort, then adjust the trial.

One hypothesis is to support preparation for, and participation in, social activities. This has not yet been confirmed as the audience's priority.

How could information reach the person?

For braille users, one possible route is accessible text on a phone connected to a braille display through the system's assistive technology. Other people may need different channels or mediation.

A vibration can serve as an alert, but it cannot convey a conversation on its own. Equipment compatibility and ways of using it need testing.
