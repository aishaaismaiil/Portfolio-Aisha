Life decisions are connected

Studying can change your working hours. Moving home can affect your budget and support network. LifeLab explores these connections through a guided life simulation, goal pathways and comparisons.

What works in this demo

  • Edit a profile and choose personal values.
  • Try decisions in guided life mode.
  • Explore goals, steps and relevant organisations.
  • Compare paths and explore the cost-of-living module.
  • Answer questions in the myths module.

Free-form simulation with AI-generated responses is not connected in this release. The demo uses guided mode. Results are simplified models, not predictions of the future or professional advice.

Try a specific task

  1. Create a fictional profile or use only data you want to store in your browser.
  2. Choose an education, work or housing decision and explore the relevant path.
  3. Tell me what made sense, where you got stuck and what you needed to take the next step.

The data you enter is stored locally in this browser. This demo has no user account or cross-device syncing.
