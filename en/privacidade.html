Contact

This website presents projects by Aisha Ismail. For questions, contact tasmiya.ismail07@gmail.com.

Browsing and external services

I have not added audience analytics or advertising tools to this portfolio. The hosting service may process technical access data. Fonts load through Google Fonts, which receives the browser's request. The pages remain readable with system fonts if that service is unavailable.

Email and testing

Contact buttons open your email app. The website does not send messages automatically. When sending feedback, include only what you wish to share and avoid sensitive personal information. You can request deletion of feedback sent to the address above.

LifeLab demo

The prototype stores your profile, progress and choices locally in your browser. This demo does not sync an account. You can use fictional data and delete it through your browser settings. The AI feature is not connected to an external service in this release.

Other websites

Stellamus opens at a separate address with its own terms and privacy page. Links to sources, universities and other services open external pages governed by their respective policies.
