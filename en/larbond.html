Asking for help is not always easy

Some people need support but cannot call or explain their situation out loud. LarBond brings together, in one place, a way to ask for help in writing, the entry points to shelter and social support, and a proposal to tackle loneliness.

What works in this demo

  • Prepare and copy a message to contact a real service, at your own pace.
  • Learn about shelter and social support resources in Portugal, such as the National Social Emergency Line (144).
  • Explore how a connection between people would work, always with consent and moderation.

LarBond does not yet offer clinical support, accommodation, emergency response or automatic referral. If you are in immediate danger, call 112. The text you write is not sent or stored on a server.

Next steps

  1. Test clarity, accessibility and privacy with students.
  2. Validate the content with partners specialised in social support.
  3. Design the connection feature, with safety and moderation rules.
