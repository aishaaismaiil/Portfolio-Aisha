Share knowledge and find common interests

Stellamus began in a university setting and connects students, former students and young people who want to learn. Its current focus is exchanging skills between peers, alongside activities, clubs and course information.

What you can explore

  • Exchanges between what you can teach and what you want to learn.
  • Activities, clubs and communities to explore.
  • Information and opinions on study paths.

Features and content may change as the platform develops and undergoes testing.
