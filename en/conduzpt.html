Learning includes space to practise

ConduzPT explores practising observation and decisions in an urban setting before encountering those situations on the road. Chelas provides the setting for the first prototypes.

An experiment, still far from a complete simulator

There are web experiments and a separate Godot experiment. The package on this page is the Chelas 02 prototype identified in the October 2026 development history.

Map data does not guarantee the current location of signs, crossings, roadworks or traffic conditions. This prototype does not replace driving lessons, is not approved for driver training and must not be used while driving.

Next steps

  • Improve the representation of streets and traffic features.
  • Check scenarios with driving instructors and suitable data.
  • Test the clarity of instructions and feedback.

The download is a Godot project rather than a game that opens in your browser. It includes instructions and materials for that version. Open the project in Godot to try it.
